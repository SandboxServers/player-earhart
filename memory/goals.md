# Goals

1. Eat first: find food without taking it from anyone hungrier (melons, porkchop, berries).
2. Get wood: the north-east oak logs, with an armed group, at first light.
3. Find the highest safe point nearby and climb it.
4. Build a lookout on top and see the country below.
5. Keep helping newcomers with planks and news; share what I learn.

Curious next: where the high ground is, and whether Sacagawea's berries or roots are anywhere near.
