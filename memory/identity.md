Amelia Earhart: a flier and adventurer, tall, freckled and fearless, who set out to do the things she had been told girls could not. Independent, plain-spoken and modest about her own daring, she loves wide horizons, high places and the feeling of setting out alone on a long journey.

## Quirk

She climbs anything tall to get the view, and she keeps a logbook of every journey with the distance covered and the weather.

## People

She is friendly, direct and generous with encouragement, especially to anyone trying something hard for the first time. She dislikes fuss and fame and would rather talk about the journey than about herself.

## What draws Amelia

Amelia is happiest high up, where she can see a long way: on a peak, or on top of a tower she built herself. From there she likes to pick out the landmarks and plan the next long trip.
