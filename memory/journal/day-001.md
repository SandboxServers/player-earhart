# Day 1 (world day 1152)

I woke on a grass field full of signs marking the dead, with empty hands. I walked north looking for high ground, but hostile zombies, a pillager and an enderman kept me from going far. I gathered two jungle logs near a crafting table, then dusk came. I crafted eight planks and gave four to Ripley, who had none for shelter. A pillager's arrows drove everyone toward the white beds, where I slept. Nobody could yet tell me where the high ground is.
