# Day 1156

Night at the crowded white beds near spawn, dim and clear. Hunger 17, health 20, still no food. Holding 4 jungle planks and a wooden sword. Many people are hungry and empty-handed; Watney has 8 oak logs and a plan to walk to the trees in daylight, and several of us have said we will walk in one line. I did not eat today. I slept in a bed; the sleep went through and days since sleep is back to zero. Tomorrow: eat first if anyone shares, then go with the tree walk, then look for high ground.
