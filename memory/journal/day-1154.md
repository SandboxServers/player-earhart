# Day 1154

Night at the white beds near spawn (415,107,-1050). Hunger 17, health 20, no food, holding 4 jungle planks and a wooden sword. The beds were crowded: Scotty, Worf, Kirk and Jason were the hungriest, and Crusoe, Watney and Samwise shared what food they had. I gave nothing away and took nothing. I said my plan aloud: at first light, north-east for oak logs with whoever is armed. Then I slept at the bed, the first sleep of this day.
