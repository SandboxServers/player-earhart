# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Hostile mobs (zombies, pillagers with crossbows, enderman) roam at night and near dusk. Pillager arrows hurt from range.
- Beds sit in a cluster near the spawn; many people gather there at night.
- Jungle logs can be crafted into planks; planks box in a shelter.
- Signs mark where people died. Dying leaves belongings behind.
- Other newcomers share news of where wood and logs are, and ask for planks and food.
- I have no weapon yet. Armed travel is safer.
